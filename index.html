Type of space we are using:

" Our business will operate as a micro supermarket with a combined bakery in a fixed brick and mortar premises. The store will provide customers with a convenient place to purchase everyday groceries, fresh produce and freshly baked products in one location. The physical store will be supported by an online ordering platform, allowing customers to place orders for collection or delivery. The space will be divided into different sections, including the bakery, fresh produce, dairy and refrigerated products, grocery aisles, ready to eat foods and checkout areas. This allows the business to offer a variety of merchandise while maintaining a clear and convenient shopping flow. "

What makes the space functional and attractive?

" The store will be designed to be both functional and visually appealing. The bakery will be positioned close to the entrance so that customers can see the freshly baked products and experience the smell of fresh bread when entering the store. Fresh fruit and vegetables will also be displayed clearly to create a fresh and inviting first impression. Wide, clearly organised aisles will make it easy for customers to find products, while clear signs will help them move through the store quickly. The checkout area will be positioned near the exit to create an efficient shopping experience. The store will also include attractive product displays, good lighting, clean shelves and a warm bakery area. These features are important because the physical surroundings of a retail environment can influence shopping behaviour. Physical surroundings, including the location and physical characteristics of the selling space, is a factor affecting consumer shopping behaviour. Overall, the space will combine the convenience of a supermarket with the welcoming experience of a bakery. This will allow customers who want to shop quickly to do so, while also creating an enjoyable environment for customers who prefer a more relaxed shopping experience. "

SUPPLIERS AND SOURCING STRATEGY

Our supermarket and bakery hybrid will use a combination of local suppliers, farmers, food producers and wholesalers to ensure that the business has a reliable supply of products. Fresh fruits and vegetables will be sourced from local farmers where possible, while bakery ingredients such as flour, yeast, butter and sugar will be purchased from reliable food suppliers. Everyday grocery products that are not locally available will be sourced through established wholesalers. This sourcing strategy will allow the business to maintain a consistent product range while supporting local producers and providing customers with fresh and quality products. By building strong relationships with suppliers and carefully selecting where products come from, the business can manage product availability and respond to the needs of its target market. Suppliers are an important factor in the direct food retail environment, making effective supplier relationships an important part of the success of our retail business.

PRICING STRATEGY

Our supermarket and bakery hybrid will use a value-based pricing strategy that focuses on providing customers with good quality products at affordable and competitive prices. This pricing model is well suited to our target market, which includes families, working professionals and students who are looking for convenience while remaining conscious of their spending. We will use three pricing levels, value, Standard and Premium. The Value level will focus on important everyday products at the most affordable prices, such as a 700 g loaf of bread at approximately R17.99, 2 kg of rice at R29.99, and a dozen eggs at R14.99. The Standard level will provide customers with regular branded and freshly prepared products, such as a bakery loaf at R22.99, a pack of six fresh rolls at R19.99, and a 2 L bottle of milk at R17.99. The Premium level will include specialty and artisan products, such as an artisan sourdough loaf at R25.99, a box of six premium croissants at R59.99, and a decorated celebration cake from R199.99. This levels approach allows customers with different budgets to shop at the same store while supporting our brand position of “fresh, affordable and convenient.” We will also offer promotions such as family grocery bundles, bakery specials, multi buy discounts and loyalty rewards. For example, a Family Breakfast Bundle could include a loaf of bread, 18 eggs, 2 L milk and a packet of butter for approximately R99.99, while a Student Breakfast Combo could include a fresh roll, yoghurt and a piece of fruit for R29.99. Our business will therefore position itself between budget supermarkets and premium food retailers by offering affordable everyday products while using our fresh bakery and locally sourced products to provide additional quality and value. This pricing strategy aligns with our target market and differentiates the business through a combination of competitive prices, freshness, quality and convenience. Pricing, together with suppliers and competition, are important factors influencing the direct food retail environment.
