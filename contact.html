Customer Service & Complaints

We want every FreshRise visit to be a good one. If something has not gone as expected, please get in touch so we can understand and respond.

Our customer service commitment

We aim to offer friendly, respectful, honest and reliable help. We will listen carefully, treat your concern with care and explain what we can do clearly.

YOUR CONCERN, TAKEN SERIOUSLY

How we handle complaints

  1. 01
    We listen

    Tell us what happened and what matters most to you.

  2. 02
    We acknowledge your concern

    We will make sure we understand the issue you have raised.

  3. 03
    We check the facts

    We will review the details you provide and any relevant information available to us.

  4. 04
    We explain fair options

    We will explain the next steps or options that may be available.

  5. 05
    We involve management when needed

    Serious or complex concerns will be referred to management.

  6. 06
    We follow up

    We will follow up on agreed next steps and share relevant updates.

PRODUCT SAFETY CONCERN

Think a product may have a food safety issue?

If you are concerned about a product, please:

  • Stop consuming the product.
  • Keep its packaging and batch or lot details, if available.
  • Contact FreshRise promptly with the details.

We will investigate the concern and provide updates. We will not assume a product caused illness before an investigation.

Urgent medical concern? Contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service.

QUICK ANSWERS

Customer service FAQ

How do I submit a complaint?

Email support@freshrisemarket.com with a description of what happened and how we can contact you.

What information should I include?

Share the product name, batch or lot number, purchase date and receipt if available, along with a brief description of your concern.

What happens after I contact FreshRise?

We will review the information, check the facts, explain any fair next steps and follow up on what we have agreed. We may contact you if we need more details.

How are food safety concerns escalated?

We will investigate the concern promptly, refer serious matters to management and provide updates. We will not draw conclusions about a product before the investigation.
